Welcome to Bristol

Big suites, a warm welcome, and all of Salalah around you.

Bristol Hotel opened in 2019 on As Saadah Street, in the busy commercial heart of Salalah. Behind the blue glass front are 42 rooms and suites, most of them with a living room and a kitchenette, so you can stay a night, a week or a whole Khareef season and still feel at home.

We’re an independent hotel, and guests notice. On Booking.com our staff score 9.0 out of 10, and the words that come up again and again are “clean”, “spacious” and “helpful”.

When to come

Salalah has two summers.

From late June to early September the Khareef brings mist, drizzle and green hills. The rest of the year is sun and a calm sea. The heated indoor pool doesn’t mind either way.

JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec
Beach Hot & dry Khareef Beach

October – March

Beach season: sunny, 27–30 °C by day and mild evenings. The best time for Al Baleed, Mughsail and boat trips.

April – June

Hot and humid, 31–34 °C. Quiet months with lower rates, and the indoor pool and spa waiting for you.

Late June – early September: the Khareef

Drizzle, mist and 24–27 °C, green hills and waterfalls, and the Salalah Tourism Festival in July and August. The busiest season, so book early.

Getting here

  • By air: Salalah International Airport (SLL) has direct flights from Muscat, Dubai, Doha, Riyadh, Jeddah and Kuwait, and from Indian cities in season. Fifteen minutes by taxi.
  • By road from Muscat: about 1,000 km on Route 31, around 10 hours’ driving. From Dubai about 1,200 km.
  • Parking is free on site for the whole stay.

How do I book?

Choose your room and dates above and tap “Send on WhatsApp”. Your request opens in WhatsApp with everything filled in. We reply with your price and confirm the room. There’s no booking fee.

Do I pay in advance?

No. You pay at the hotel when you arrive. Cash is always accepted; ask us on WhatsApp if you’d like to pay by card. Please bring photo ID for check-in.

Can I cancel?

Message us on WhatsApp before you confirm and we’ll explain the cancellation terms for your dates. If your plans change later, tell us as early as you can.

Do the rooms have a kitchen?

The Junior, Superior and Executive Suites have a kitchenette with a fridge, microwave and kettle, plus a dining table. The Deluxe Room has a fridge and a tea and coffee maker.

Is there a discount for a week or a month?

Yes. Many of our guests stay for weeks at a time, especially in the Khareef. Tell us your dates on WhatsApp and we’ll quote a weekly or monthly rate.

Can you pick me up from the airport?

Yes. We arrange paid transfers from Salalah International Airport, about 15 minutes away. Send us your flight number on WhatsApp.

Arriving late?

Check-in runs until 11:00 pm and the front desk is open 24 hours. If you’ll be later, message us your arrival time and we’ll be ready for you.

Can children use the pool?

Yes. The indoor pool is heated and there is a shallow children’s pool next to it. Children must be with an adult.

Can you decorate the room for a honeymoon or a birthday?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we’ll have the room ready before you arrive.

Is the hotel suitable for families with small children?

Yes. The suites have a living room and a kitchenette, and there is a shallow children’s pool. We don’t have cots, so bring a travel cot if you need one.

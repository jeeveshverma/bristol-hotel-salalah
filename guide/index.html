Sample plans

Two days, four days or one.

Use these as a starting point. We’ll book the car and adjust the plan to the weather on the day.

2 days · late June – early September

Good to know

Before you set off.

Getting around

There are no tour buses between the sights. We arrange a car with a driver, a taxi or a hire car, usually the same day.

Mountain roads in the Khareef

Fog can cut visibility to a few metres. Drive slowly with your lights on, and expect camels on the road.

What to wear

Light clothes, a rain jacket in the Khareef, and covered shoulders and knees at mosques and in villages.

The sea

The sea is rough during the Khareef and swimming is often not allowed. In winter, swim where it is calm and others are swimming.

Fuel and water

Fill up before the desert and the far east of the coast, where stations are far apart, and carry water.

We’re here to help

Message us before you set off and we’ll tell you what’s open, what the weather is doing and how long it takes.
